    How to play

    Make words from the seven letters. Find as many as you can.

    • Words need at least four letters.
    • Every word must use the center letter.
    • Letters can be used more than once.
    • No proper nouns, no hyphenated words, nothing obscure or offensive. There is never an S.

    Scoring

    • A four-letter word is worth 1 point.
    • Longer words are worth 1 point per letter.
    • A septet uses all seven letters and earns 7 extra points. Every puzzle has at least one.

    Your points move you up through the ranks, from Tuning Up to Maestro. Tap your rank to see what each one takes.

    Playing

    Tap the letters or type. Enter submits, Backspace deletes, Space shuffles. A new puzzle arrives every day at midnight, and your progress is saved on this device.
